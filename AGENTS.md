# AGENTS.md

Instructions for AI agents working in this repository.

## Source of truth

The official hackathon challenges live in `hackathon_challenges.md`. When helping with this project, you must:

- Treat `hackathon_challenges.md` as the authoritative list of challenges.
- Use it when choosing or comparing challenges, brainstorming solutions, scoping an MVP, or drafting pitches and documentation.
- Work only from the four problem statements and their "Extracted Elements" summaries.
- Not invent requirements, constraints, users, or data sources that are not stated there.
- Remember that anything under "Extracted Elements" is a summary of the problem statement, not new information.

## The four official challenges

1. **Clean Energy Adoption and Rewards** — Help customers compare solar options, and recognize and reward sustainable energy choices, using clear, comparable and verifiable information.
2. **Know Your Bill, Manage Your Usage** — Help customers understand what makes up their electricity bill and use their own consumption data to manage their usage.
3. **Grid Intelligence from Connected Devices** — Combine data from smart meters, IoT sensors, field devices, other data-gathering tools, and publicly available online information to understand how the distribution network is behaving (changing demand such as EV charging, unusual patterns indicating equipment issues or safety hazards, and confirming and locating possible power issues).
4. **Smarter Field Observation** — Use field assets such as utility vehicles, dashcams, smartphones and low-cost environmental sensors to safely capture useful environmental and infrastructure data.

## Working rules

- Before proposing a solution, name which challenge (or challenges) it addresses.
- Keep proposals within the stated users, goals, data sources, and constraints of the chosen challenge.
- If the user's request does not map to any of the four challenges, say so explicitly.
- For challenges 3 and 4, note that primary users are inferred from context, not named in the source text.
- When in doubt, quote the relevant problem statement from `hackathon_challenges.md` rather than paraphrasing into new requirements.
